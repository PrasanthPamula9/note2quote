Note2Quote Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Note2Quote collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the app. It is written to reflect the app's current behavior as of the effective date below.

App Note2Quote
Effective date June 10, 2026
Contact prasanthpamula9@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide. You may enter the following information in the app:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Instagram handle
  • Quote text and related content
  • Images you choose from your device, camera, or Unsplash

At this time, profile fields are used only to display author information inside quotes. We do not use your email address for login, marketing, or account creation.

Information stored locally on your device. The app stores certain information locally, including:

  • Saved quotes
  • Editor settings
  • Your profile information
  • Unsplash search cache
  • App preferences and related content

This data remains on your device unless you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described in this policy to:

  • Create and edit quotes
  • Display the author name in quote designs
  • Save quotes and settings locally
  • Let you choose images from your device or Unsplash
  • Export or share created quote images
  • Show advertisements through a third-party ad network
  • Improve the app's functionality and user experience

3. Unsplash Images and Search

The app includes an Unsplash image picker. When you use Unsplash:

  • Your search terms are sent to Unsplash.
  • The app loads photo results from Unsplash.
  • The app sends a download tracking request to Unsplash when you select a photo, as required by Unsplash's API guidelines.
  • The app includes attribution information and links to the photographer profile and photo page.

Unsplash may collect and process information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

4. Advertising

The app uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process certain information, which may include device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, approximate location, diagnostic information, and other data used for ad delivery and measurement, depending on your settings and region.

If required by law, consent may be requested for ad personalization or related processing.

5. Permissions We Use

The app may request access to the following permissions depending on what you choose to do:

  • Camera, to take a photo for a quote background
  • Photos or media library, to select an image from your device
  • Storage or file access, to save or export quote images

These permissions are used only when you actively choose the related feature.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only in these situations:

  • With Unsplash, when you use the Unsplash image search and selection features
  • With Google and its advertising partners, when ads are shown in the app
  • If required by law, legal process, or government request
  • To protect our rights, users, or the app from fraud, abuse, or security threats

7. Data Retention

Because the app currently stores most user content locally on your device:

  • Your data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app.
  • Cached Unsplash results may remain locally to improve performance.
  • If you delete the app or clear its data, locally stored information may be removed.

If future cloud sync or account features are added, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are used.

8. Your Choices

You can:

  • Edit or remove your profile details inside the app
  • Delete saved quotes from the app
  • Clear app data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored information
  • Manage ad-related privacy choices where available on your device or in applicable settings

9. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for children under the age of 13, or the minimum age required by law in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Unsplash
  • Google Mobile Ads

These services have their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review them.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information handled by the app, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date and may provide notice inside the app or by other appropriate means.
